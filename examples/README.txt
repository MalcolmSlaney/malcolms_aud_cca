Two cross-checks of the python port against de Cheveigne's NoiseTools.

  synthetic/    9 matrices, 400x4 to 3000x24, including rank-deficient,
                ill-conditioned, pure noise and perfectly correlated views
  eeg/          subject 5 run 1 of the Natural Speech set, raw and unfiltered,
                against the envelope of the audiobook that was played
  eeg/models/   leave-one-out models from paper_loo.py: per-fold weights at the
                best shift, the score-vs-shift curves, and loo_scores.csv

report.txt in each folder is the result of the last run.

run order (same for either folder), from inside this directory

  cd .../examples
  python synthetic/gen.py
  matlab -batch "nt_ref synthetic"
  python check.py synthetic          ->  synthetic/report.txt

data.mat and nt_out.mat are not in git. Both are regenerated by the two steps
above, and the eeg one would mean shipping the recordings, which belong to the
Dryad release rather than here. eeg/gen.py reads them from the path at the top
of the file, so point that at your own copy of the Natural Speech set.

two paths come from the environment, with a sensible default next to the repo:

  NOISETOOLS        the NoiseTools folder            (default ../NoiseTools)
  NATURAL_SPEECH    your copy of the Natural Speech  (default ../data/Natural Speech)

data.mat carries the inputs and the settings both sides read, so MATLAB and
python can never see different numbers.
